About

A media buyer who thinks in P&Ls.

I didn't come to paid ads from design or copy. I came from building financial models — daily cash flow, per-client P&L, margin by channel, the works.

So when I run an ad account, I'm not chasing a vanity ROAS. I'm watching contribution margin, CAC payback and cash flow — because that's what decides whether spending more tomorrow is a smart move or a slow leak.

I work remotely with US and EU teams, close the loop on the numbers that matter, and report in plain financial language. No screenshot dumps. No "trust me, it's working."

Straight answers

Before you book.

What access do you need to start?
Read access to your ad accounts and analytics, and a short call on your unit economics — COGS, fulfillment, average order or lead value. Nothing I can't be given in a day, and nothing that lets me spend a dollar you didn't approve.
How do you charge?
A flat monthly retainer scoped to your spend and complexity — no percentage-of-spend, so my incentive is your profit, not your budget getting bigger. We agree the number before we start.
What if you can't move the number?
Then I'll tell you on the intro call, before you pay anything. The diagnostic comes first: if the account is already near its ceiling or the economics don't support paid, you'll hear that straight rather than a pitch.
How fast do results show up?
Diagnostics and cleanup land in the first couple of weeks. Meaningful movement in contribution usually takes a full test cycle — think 30 to 60 days — because I scale on evidence, not on a hunch.
